ATTENDANCE POLICY

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent during the semester. Students who fail to maintain the required attendance may not be permitted to appear for the semester examination. Attendance is calculated based on the classes conducted during the semester.

EXAMINATION POLICY

Semester examinations are conducted twice during the academic year. Students must complete examination registration before the specified deadline. Students must carry their valid identity card when attending examinations.

HOSTEL POLICY

Students staying in the hostel must follow the hostel rules and regulations. Hostel entry and exit timings must be followed. Students leaving the hostel after the permitted time may require prior permission from the hostel authorities.

PLACEMENT POLICY

Students applying for campus placements must satisfy the eligibility requirements specified by the recruiting company. Eligibility may depend on academic performance, backlogs, attendance, and other requirements.

LIBRARY POLICY

Students can borrow books using their valid student identity card. Books must be returned before the due date. Late returns may result in penalties according to the library rules.
